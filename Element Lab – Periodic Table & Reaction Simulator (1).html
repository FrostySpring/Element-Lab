How does this simulation work?

Particles move at speeds set by the temperature and bounce off each other. A reaction happens only when a collision carries more energy than the activation energy (Eₐ). Heat released by a reaction speeds the particles up, and the walls pull the gas back toward the heat-bath temperature. Energies are scaled down so the effect is visible on screen. Shortcuts: Space pauses, R resets, I ignites.
